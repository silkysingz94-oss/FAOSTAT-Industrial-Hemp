Methodology and limitations
This dashboard displays observations retrieved from FAOSTAT. A missing country observation is not interpreted as zero production. Hemp-related commodity records and statistical indicators may have different definitions and units. Do not sum different indicators or units. The selected year is 2024; the latest available observation may differ by country.
Data provenance

Source: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), FAOSTAT. Domain: QCL – Crops and livestock products. Reference year: 2024. Boundaries: Natural Earth / World Atlas.
